## K05 selection and limitations

The previous staircase (Todd Quackenbush / JJB_K8aCPU4), stepped facade (Julien Moreau / 688Fna1pwOQ) and historic doorway (Paul Evans / CtkDsu4w-Rs) are preserved in the historical K04 screenshots and Git history. They are no longer served by the page. The replacements show the relationships described by the concepts: sheltered planting and pale walls; a low roof and a slope; timber shelter among trees. The source pages identify all three as free under the Unsplash License; each image was inspected before selection.

These are references to other residences, not depictions of the fictional KILN designs. The courtyard source identifies Haniffa Residence by RMJA / RMJ Architects in Colombo. The ridge reference supports the slope and roof relationship, without establishing the imagined floor plan. The woodland reference shows an A-frame cabin, while the concept text does not prescribe a roof shape. Final matching views remain the four ImageGen images scheduled for K08–K09. No final image generation was performed in K05.
